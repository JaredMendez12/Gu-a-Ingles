3. Presente simple

Úsalo para hábitos, rutinas, hechos y horarios. Con he, she, it el verbo lleva -s.

FormaI / you / we / theyhe / she / it
AfirmativaI work from home.She works from home.
NegativaI don't work on Sundays.She doesn't work on Sundays.
PreguntaDo you work here?Does she work here?
Respuesta cortaYes, I do. / No, I don't.Yes, she does. / No, she doesn't.
Regla de la -sEjemplos
General: + splay → plays · read → reads
Termina en -s, -sh, -ch, -x, -o: + eswatch → watches · go → goes · do → does · fix → fixes
Consonante + y: y → iesstudy → studies · try → tries (pero play → plays)
Irregularhave → has

Usos

  • Hábitos y rutinas: I go to the gym three times a week.
  • Hechos generales: Water boils at 100 °C.
  • Horarios: The train leaves at 8:15.
  • Estados (verbos que no van en continuo): I know, I like, I need, I understand.

Expresiones de tiempo: always, usually, often, sometimes, never, every day, on Mondays, twice a month, in the morning.

Ojo: después de does / doesn't el verbo va sin -s: Does she work? (no Does she works?).

4. Presente continuo y verbos de estado

Usa am / is / are + verbo-ing para lo que pasa ahora, lo temporal y los planes ya acordados.

FormaEjemplos
AfirmativaI'm working. · She's working. · They're working.
NegativaI'm not working. · She isn't working. · They aren't working.
PreguntaAre you working? · Is he working?
Regla del -ingEjemplos
General: + ingwork → working · study → studying
Termina en e muda: quita la emake → making · write → writing (pero see → seeing)
Consonante-vocal-consonante con sílaba fuerte: doblarun → running · stop → stopping · begin → beginning
Termina en -ie: → ylie → lying · die → dying

Usos

  • Ahora mismo: I'm writing an email right now.
  • Temporal: She's living with her aunt this semester.
  • Cambio o tendencia: Prices are going up.
  • Futuro acordado: I'm meeting Ana tomorrow at 5.
  • Queja con always: He's always arriving late!
Presente simple (hábito, siempre)Presente continuo (ahora, temporal)
I work at a bank.I'm working from home today.
He plays soccer on Sundays.He's playing soccer right now.

Verbos de estado (casi nunca van en -ing): know, understand, believe, like, love, hate, want, need, prefer, seem, belong, own, mean, remember, forget, cost.

  • No digas I'm knowing, sino I know.
  • think: I think it's good (opinión) / I'm thinking about it (proceso).
  • have: I have a car (posesión) / I'm having lunch (acción).

5. Pasado simple

Úsalo para acciones terminadas en un momento del pasado. Los regulares agregan -ed y los irregulares se memorizan (documento 03).

FormaEjemplos
AfirmativaI worked yesterday. · She went to Cancún.
NegativaI didn't work. · She didn't go.
PreguntaDid you work? · Did she go?
Respuesta cortaYes, I did. · No, she didn't.

Clave: did / didn't + verbo en forma base: Did you see it? (no Did you saw it?).

Usos

  • Acción terminada con tiempo definido: I finished the project last night.
  • Serie de acciones: I woke up, took a shower and left.
  • Hábitos del pasado: When I was a kid, I played outside every day.

Expresiones de tiempo: yesterday, last week / month / year, two days ago, in 2019, when I was a child, the day before yesterday.

6. Pasado continuo

Usa was / were + -ing para una acción en progreso en el pasado, sobre todo cuando otra acción la interrumpe.

FormaEjemplos
AfirmativaI / he / she / it was working · you / we / they were working
NegativaI wasn't working · they weren't working
PreguntaWas she working? · Were you working?

Usos

  • En progreso a una hora concreta: At 8 p.m. I was studying.
  • Interrumpida por otra acción (when + pasado simple): I was cooking when the phone rang.
  • Dos acciones al mismo tiempo (while): While I was coding, my brother was playing.
  • Ambiente de una historia: The sun was shining and people were walking in the park.

7. Presente perfecto y presente perfecto continuo

El presente perfecto (have / has + participio) une el pasado con el presente: experiencias, resultados y acciones que todavía siguen.

FormaEjemplos
AfirmativaI / you / we / they have finished · he / she / it has finished
NegativaI haven't finished · she hasn't finished
PreguntaHave you finished? · Has he finished?
UsoPalabras claveEjemplo
Experiencias sin fechaever, never, beforeHave you ever been to Canada? I've never tried sushi.
Acciones que empezaron y siguenfor, sinceI've lived here for 5 years / since 2021.
Pasado reciente con efecto ahorajust, already, yetI've just finished. She hasn't arrived yet. Have you already eaten?
Periodo que no ha terminadotoday, this week, so farI've drunk three coffees today.

for o since

  • for + duración: for 2 hours, for 3 years, for a long time.
  • since + punto de inicio: since 8 a.m., since Monday, since 2020, since I was a child.
Presente perfecto (sin fecha o sigue)Pasado simple (con fecha, terminado)
I've been to Cancún.I went to Cancún in 2023.
She has lost her keys (aún no aparecen).She lost her keys yesterday.
I've worked here since 2022 (sigo aquí).I worked there from 2019 to 2021.
Have you seen my phone?Did you see the game last night?

Presente perfecto continuo (have / has been + -ing)

  • Duración de una acción que sigue: I've been studying English for two years.
  • Actividad reciente con resultado visible: I'm tired because I've been running.
  • Pregunta típica: How long have you been waiting?
  • Simple = resultado (I've written three emails); continuo = actividad o duración (I've been writing emails all morning).

8. Pasado perfecto (y pasado perfecto continuo)

Usa had + participio para la acción que ocurrió antes de otra acción pasada: es «el pasado del pasado».

FormaEjemplos
AfirmativaI / you / he / she / we / they had finished
NegativaThey hadn't finished
PreguntaHad you finished?

Ejemplos

  • When I arrived, the class had already started. (Primero empezó la clase; después llegué.)
  • She had never seen snow before she went to Canada.
  • I couldn't log in because I had forgotten my password.

Compara

  • When I arrived, the class started. → Llegué y en ese momento empezó.
  • When I arrived, the class had started. → Ya había empezado cuando llegué.

Pasado perfecto continuo (had been + -ing): duración antes de un momento pasado.

  • I had been waiting for an hour when the bus finally came.
  • His eyes were red because he had been crying.

Palabras clave: already, before, after, by the time, when, until, never... before.

9. Futuro: will, going to y presente continuo

No hay una sola forma de futuro: will para decisiones del momento y predicciones, going to para planes y presente continuo para citas acordadas.

willPredicción u opiniónI think it will rain.
willPromesa, ofrecimiento, pedidoI'll help you. Will you call me later?
be going to + basePlan o intención ya decididaI'm going to study medicine.
be going toPredicción con evidenciaLook at those clouds! It's going to rain.
Presente continuoCita o arreglo con fechaI'm seeing the dentist on Friday.
Presente simpleHorarios oficialesThe flight leaves at 6:40.

Negativas y preguntas: I won't forget. · Will you come? · She's not going to come. · Are you going to buy it?

Tiempo (B2)EstructuraEjemplo
Futuro continuowill be + -ingThis time tomorrow I'll be flying to Madrid.
Futuro perfectowill have + participioBy 2030, I'll have finished my master's.

Ojo: después de when, if, as soon as, before y after se usa presente, no will: I'll call you when I arrive (no when I will arrive).

11. Resumen: los 12 tiempos y cómo elegir

Los 12 tiempos combinan 3 momentos (presente, pasado y futuro) con 4 aspectos (simple, continuo, perfecto y perfecto continuo).

TiempoEstructuraEjemploUso principal
Presente simplebase / -sI work.Hábitos, hechos
Presente continuoam / is / are + -ingI'm working.Ahora, temporal
Presente perfectohave / has + participioI have worked.Experiencia, resultado, algo que sigue
Presente perfecto continuohave / has been + -ingI have been working.Duración hasta ahora
Pasado simplepasadoI worked.Terminado, con fecha
Pasado continuowas / were + -ingI was working.En progreso en el pasado
Pasado perfectohad + participioI had worked.Antes de otro pasado
Pasado perfecto continuohad been + -ingI had been working.Duración antes de otro pasado
Futuro simplewill + baseI will work.Decisión, predicción
Futuro continuowill be + -ingI will be working.En progreso en el futuro
Futuro perfectowill have + participioI will have worked.Terminado antes de un momento futuro
Futuro perfecto continuowill have been + -ingI will have been working.Duración hasta un momento futuro

A la izquierda de AHORA está lo que ya pasó; las rayas marcan el otro momento con el que se compara la acción (por ejemplo, when I arrived o by 2030).

Cómo elegir el tiempo en 6 preguntas

  1. ¿Es un hábito o un hecho? → presente simple.
  2. ¿Está pasando ahora o es temporal? → presente continuo.
  3. ¿Terminó y dices cuándo? → pasado simple.
  4. ¿Es una experiencia sin fecha o algo que sigue hasta hoy? → presente perfecto.
  5. ¿Pasó antes de otra acción pasada? → pasado perfecto.
  6. ¿Es un plan ya decidido? → going to. ¿Lo decides en el momento? → will.
