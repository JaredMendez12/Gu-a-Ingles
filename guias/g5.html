Cómo se dicen

  • 13 vs 30: cambia la sílaba fuerte: thirTEEN / THIRty. Igual 14/40, 15/50, etc.
  • 145: one hundred (and) forty-five. El and es común en el Reino Unido.
  • Miles y decimales: la coma separa miles y el punto, decimales: 1,500.75 = one thousand five hundred point seven five.
  • Años: 1998 = nineteen ninety-eight · 2008 = two thousand eight · 2026 = twenty twenty-six.
  • Teléfonos: dígito por dígito; el 0 se dice oh o zero.
  • Porcentajes y fracciones: 25 % = twenty-five percent · 1/2 = a half · 1/3 = a third · 3/4 = three quarters.
  • Dinero: $250 = two hundred fifty dollars · $3.50 = three fifty.

Fechas

  • Estados Unidos escribe mes/día/año; Reino Unido, día/mes/año. 05/03/2026 es May 3 en EE. UU. y 5 March en Reino Unido.
  • Se dicen con ordinal: May 3rd = «May third» · the 5th of March = «the fifth of March».
  • Días y meses van con mayúscula: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday · January … December.
  • Preposiciones: in November · on November 21st · in 2026.

Conectores (linking words)

Los conectores unen ideas y hacen que hables y escribas a nivel B1–B2; apréndelos por función.

FunciónConectoresEjemplo
Añadirand, also, too, as well, in addition, moreover, furthermore, besidesThe app is fast. Moreover, it's free.
Contrastarbut, however, although, even though, though, while, whereas, despite / in spite of, on the other hand, neverthelessAlthough it was late, we kept working.
Causabecause, since, as, because of, due toWe stayed home because of the rain.
Consecuenciaso, therefore, as a result, consequently, that's whyIt was raining, so we stayed home.
Finalidadto, in order to, so thatI study in order to get a better job.
Ejemplofor example, for instance, such as, likeLanguages such as Python and Java...
Secuenciafirst, then, next, after that, finally, meanwhileFirst, install the app. Then, log in.
Condiciónif, unless, as long as, in case, otherwiseSave the file; otherwise, you'll lose it.
Conclusiónin conclusion, to sum up, overall, in shortTo sum up, practice every day.
Aclararin other words, that is, I meanIn other words, it isn't ready.

Gramática y puntuación

  • However, therefore, moreover suelen ir al inicio de una oración nueva, seguidos de coma: It's expensive. However, it's worth it.
  • Although + oración completa; despite + sustantivo o -ing: Although it rained... / Despite the rain... / Despite being tired...
  • Because + oración; because of + sustantivo: because it rained / because of the rain.
  • Eventually no es «eventualmente»: significa «al final» (ver falsos amigos).

Acento de palabra (word stress)

  • La sílaba fuerte cambia el significado: REcord (registro) / reCORD (grabar) · PREsent (regalo) / preSENT (presentar).
  • Familias que se mueven: PHOtograph → phoTOgraphy → photoGRAphic.
  • Con -tion y -ic, la sílaba fuerte es la anterior: inforMAtion, ecoNOMic.
  • Palabras que suelen pronunciarse mal: deVElop · comfortable (/ˈkʌmftəbəl/) · vegetable (/ˈvedʒtəbəl/) · Wednesday (/ˈwenzdeɪ/) · women (/ˈwɪmɪn/) · clothes (/kloʊðz/).

Letras mudas: knife, know (k) · write, wrong (w) · island (s) · listen, castle (t) · hour, honest (h) · half, walk, talk, could, should (l) · receipt (p) · debt, doubt (b).

Habla conectada (informal): want to → wanna · going to → gonna · got to → gotta · did you → didja · what are you → whatcha. Entiéndelas al escuchar, pero no las escribas en textos formales.

Cómo practicar 10 minutos al día

  1. Busca la palabra en el Cambridge Dictionary y escucha el audio de EE. UU. y Reino Unido.
  2. Búscala en YouGlish para oírla en videos reales.
  3. Grábate diciendo 5 pares mínimos y compáralos con el audio.
  4. Haz shadowing de una frase completa, imitando ritmo y entonación.
